    How it works

    1. Draft. A small LLM, behind a Cloudflare Worker so the key never reaches the browser, turns the question into a JSON filter. It knows the two teams and the periods, but not the roster or a single event.
    2. Check. TypeScript resolves every value against the game: players by name, number or a close typo, event types, periods, clock times, strength. Anything that doesn't exist stops the search and says why, with a "did you mean" fix. Auto-corrections are shown, never silent.
    3. Search. Plain filtering over the game's events. Counts, xG totals and the rink come from the data only.
    4. Video sync.
